# NTPC Credit Report

## Project Overview
A four-year credit and financial analysis of NTPC Limited covering FY 2022-23 to FY 2025-26.

## Objective
To evaluate NTPC's creditworthiness, financial strength, debt repayment capacity, liquidity, profitability, and major credit risks.

## Key Areas Analysed
- Credit Ratings
- Financial Ratios
- Profitability
- Liquidity
- Capital Structure
- Debt Servicing Capacity
- Working Capital
- Credit Risks

## Key Findings
- Strong domestic credit ratings of AAA/Stable and A1+
- Debt-equity ratio improved from 1.34x to 1.09x
- DSCR improved from 1.29x to 1.68x
- Interest coverage improved from 3.19x to 4.75x
- Net profit margin improved from 10.50% to 14.00%
- Overall credit risk assessed as low

## Key Risks
- DISCOM payment delays
- Interest rate risk
- Foreign exchange risk
- High capital expenditure
- Project execution risk

## Credit Recommendation
Credit can be approved/continued, subject to regular monitoring of liquidity, receivables, debt servicing capacity, and project execution.
